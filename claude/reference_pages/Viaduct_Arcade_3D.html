North arch viaduct: the arcade

The north arch viaduct from just north of Bank to past Harrison Avenue (about 465 m), at real size on the Deck's track and street layout. The rail is 7.4 m above the streets. Each street passes under a girder bridge hidden behind an arch, so the arcade reads as continuous from the side. The blocks between hold shops cut into the embankment, fronted by pedestrian streets, and masonry pylons carry the catenary.

Tracks, structure edges, streets and ground are the Deck's (published layout in the hub repo) and the map 3 ground. Ten tracks on the deck: ORR / Transvalley west pair, APEx Indianapolis, Dayton and Norwood pairs, ORR / Transvalley east pair, all at 4.5 m. The model is cut just north of Bank, where the lower pairs start their dive.

Dolph, McRoberts and Western used to bend under the east side of the viaduct, where the Deck's streets meet the basin's. They now run straight through and bend about 10 m beyond the east face, so each opening is the same width on both faces (Ben, 28 October).

Not to scale from the mockup (Ben). Drawing values: piers 2.0 to 4.0 m, bays fitted to stay between about 16.5 and 19.3 m, sidewalks 3.4 m, the bus 0.25 m off the kerb, the catenary heights (contact wire 6.4 m, messenger 7.3 m, girder 7.7 to 8.6 m over the rail), the frontage street section, three shop units per arch.
