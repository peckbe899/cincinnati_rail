The twin tubes run in opposite directions: the near tube toward the far end of the stretch, the far tube back. Third rails on the inner sides of twin tubes and the outer sides of the double bore. The equipment spacing is the same as the box cutaway: lights every 18 m, hose valves every 61 m, transponders every 100 m (assumed), one radio access point, one blue light station, one cross passage (at most 244 m apart), distance markers every 100 m, a signal and trip stop for each track. The rings are left whole over the last 40 m.

Sources: as on the Metro Tube and Box Sections page; ring width from the Brunel cast-iron ring paper.
