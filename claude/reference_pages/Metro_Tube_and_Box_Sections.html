APhase 1 shallow shield tube

Type A. Cast-iron rings, timber sleepers set in concrete with a centre drain, rail 0.70 m above the invert. The low bench walls each side carry duct banks, as the 1904 IRT did in its side walls, and give a ledge to walk out on. Legacy signals and trip stops stay; the leaky feeder, transponders and radio access points were added for CBTC.

BDeep tube in the hills

The same type A section, driven 25–45 m down. Depth changes the stations (elevators), the grout behind the rings, the shafts (draught relief alongside the fan shafts) and the drainage (a sump at every low point), not the tube.

CPhase 2, option 1: one double-track bore open decision

The canon rule of 9 October (one bore, after the Holland Tunnel). Sized to the dynamic envelope, two tracks at 3.90 m need 8.06 m, so the canon 9.0 m bore fits with a cast-iron lining. The deep invert is mass concrete carrying duct banks. Third rails on the outer sides, signals on the outer walls.

DPhase 2, option 2: type A twin tubes open decision

Same tube as Phase 1, with 3.24 m of ground between the tubes. Cross passages at bench level with fire doors, at most 244 m apart (NFPA 130), would have been added when GoA3 came in.

EPhase 3 and later tube

Type C. Concrete segments 0.25 m thick, slab track on an invert fill with a centre drain. The walkway is LA Metro's envelope (0.76 × 2.03 m, surface at car-floor height 1.12 m) on the side away from the third rail, with cable troughs under it and a handrail. The tube centre sits 0.17 m toward the walkway.

FDouble-track box

Cut-and-cover. Track centres 3.90 m (your 26 October value) with steel columns between the tracks, about 0.25 m square and 1.52 m (5 ft) apart as on the 1904 IRT, 2.0 m from each track centre to the wall for racks, lights and signals. 1.0 m below the rail and 4.6 m to the roof top, as canon.

GIsland station box

8.0 m island, edges 1.45 m from the track centres, platform at car-floor height (1.12 m), 4.6 m from rail to roof underside. Platform-edge intrusion detection is the GoA3 addition here.

HIsland station approach, in plan

Lengthwise to scale, side to side four times. Type A tubes run 8.74 m apart and ease out 1.08 m each to 10.9 m before the box. Type C tubes run 9.14 m apart and ease 0.88 m. Tracks only come closer than the tube spacing inside a box.

IEquipment along a kilometre between two platforms

Lengthwise to scale. 0 is the end of station 1’s platform and 1000 the start of station 2’s; both stations sit outside the kilometre. Lights every 18 m (1904 IRT), hose outlets every 61 m (200 ft), blue light stations every 250 m (about the 800 ft WMATA spacing) and at each platform end, four cross passages 200 m apart (NFPA 130 allows up to 244 m), distance markers every 100 m, leaky feeder fed at 100 m and 900 m (at most 800 m apart), a sump at the low point, vent shafts at both ends of each station. Assumed: 250 m signal blocks with a signal and trip stop at each platform end facing the tunnel, transponders every 100 m plus one at each stopping point, radio access points about every 300 m, intrusion detection on the last 100 m into each platform and along the platform edges (faint).
