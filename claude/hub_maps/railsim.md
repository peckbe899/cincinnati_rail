## 1. railsim entry points (`railsim/`, 6,997 lines, v10.0.0)

- **Programs:**
  - `python -m railsim.metro CFG`: `metro.py:126`, `SPEC` with `data/rollingstock/metro.yaml`.
  - `python -m railsim.commuter CFG`: `commuter.py:159`, with `PRESETS` commuter, intercity and hsr at `commuter.py:68-101`.
  - `railsim.train` is an alias for commuter.
  - `python -m railsim.batch FOLDER --pattern` (`batch.py`) writes `output/batch_summary.csv`.
- **Shared runner:** `app.main(spec, argv)` (`app.py:278-438`). It is argparse-only. Flags: `--train --load --direction --rail dry|wet|leaf --out --no-plots --monte-carlo N --headway --year --stops --list-trains`.
  - It returns `(route, [(DirectionResult, summary_dict)])`, but it always prints, and it writes `output/<stem>/` unless `--out` is given.
  - There is no pure-function API. `tests/test_calibration.py` shells out and then reads `output/<stem>/summary.json`.
- **Config merge order:** program defaults < preset < era overlay < line config (`app.py:304-318`, `deep_merge` at `app.py:52-68`). A unit-suffixed key replaces its siblings (`_mph`, `_kmh`, `_ms`, `_in`, `_mm`, `_ft`, `_m`). The SI readers are `line.speed_of` and `line.length_of`.
- **Lower-level API already used by tests:** `tests/test_core.py` builds `TrackData` by hand and runs `Simulator(train, TrackData, Driver).run([Stop...])`. That is the narrowest seam (section 7).

## 3. How a route is built (`line.build_route`, `line.py:186-574`)

1. **KMZ:** `kml.read_kmz`. A `Placemark` has `name, folder_path, points[(lat,lon,z)], lines, has_z`. `select(folder, name_regex)` is at `kml.py:151`. `data/kmz/Cincinnati_Rail.kmz` is 223 KB: 859 placemarks, 77 lines, 771 points, no z values.
2. **Projection:** `geo.LocalProjection.around(mean lat, mean lon)` (`geo.py:28-62`), a WGS84 local tangent plane. **Each route gets its own origin**; there is no region frame.
3. **Track network:** `network.Network.build(polys, names, snap_tol)`, then `route(stops, max_turn, reverse_at)` → `(drawn_xy, stop_ch)`, plus `last_route_ranges` (segment name, s0, s1) and `last_route_pieces`.
4. **Horizontal:** `horizontal.fit_horizontal_pieces(xy, CurveDesign, breaks, sketch_ranges)` → `HorizontalAlignment{xy (~2 m in curves), s, curves[Curve{direction, deflection, radius, spiral, s_ts, s_sc, s_cs, s_st, tangent, cant, vertices}], drawn_length}` (`horizontal.py:84-144`).
5. **Stations:** projected onto the fitted line → `StationInfo{name, lat, lon, s, offset, cls, platform, platform_length, dwell_override, early_departure, stop, reverse, psd}` (`line.py:58-73`). Stopping patterns, skips and loop services are at 316-336.
6. **Ground:** the first source that works wins (`elevation.py`):
   - `from_csv`, `from_kmz_lines`;
   - `from_dem`: a local raster via rasterio/pyproj, e.g. `ground/2026/map3_profile_dem_dm.vrt`, scale 0.1, with a `_water.tif` overlay;
   - `from_lidar`: USGS TNM 1 m via `/vsicurl/` to prd-tnm.s3, network, cached in `data/elevation_cache/lidar_*.json`;
   - `from_terrain_tiles`: AWS Terrarium z13, network, cached;
   - `from_usgs`: EPQS, network.
   - If all fail, the profile is flat, with a warning.
7. **Vertical:** `profile.design_profile` (scipy `lsq_linear`), or for analyzer configs the designed profile:
   - `metro_profile.route_profile` (Metro: replaces the profile, 5 m grid, `metro_profile.py:2235`);
   - `overlay_profile` (RER: the core laid over the ground fit with a 400 m taper, `:2260`).
   - Either way the result is a `Profile{s, ground, track, grade, structure[str]}`.
   - Analyzer types map elevated→viaduct, atgrade→at-grade, trough→cut, cutcover→cut-and-cover, shield, deep→deep-tube, sunk→sunk-box. `TUNNEL_STRUCTURES` (`metro_profile.py:48`) switches on tunnel drag.
   - `MetroProfile.load` also reads hub/seed params (metro_structure, chronology, platforms, complexes, access) through module-level `_pick` constants (`:117-126`).
8. **Speed:** `speed.build_speed_profile(h, SpeedPolicy, extra)` (`speed.py:132`) gives a curve limit from R, cant, spiral and deflection, quantised to the increment or code steps, with short zones merged.
   - Extra `Restriction{s0, s1, speed, reason}` comes from config restrictions, turnouts (`operations.turnout_speed`, AREMA and DB tables), terminal approaches, station throats, and through or passing platforms.
   - Reason strings (`"curve R=..."`, `"<stn> station throat"`, ...) are the only attribution keys.
9. **Route** (`line.py:76-138`): adds `supply_zones`, `neutral_sections`, `zone_ranges`, `drawn_ranges`, `sketch_ranges`.
   - `Route.track_data(ds=2.0)` → `TrackData{ds, s, limit, elevation, curvature, tunnel, power_factor, no_traction, zone}` (`dynamics.py:37-68`).

## 4. Simulation and outputs

- **Directions:** `app.run_direction` (`app.py:176-188`) reverses the `TrackData`. `_stops_for` sets stop positions: `far_end` puts the head 5 m short of the platform end; stub reversals use a `jump` of the train length + 6 m, with a minimum dwell of 240 s.
- **Simulator:** `Simulator(train, td, driver, zone_drivers).run([Stop{s, name, dwell, jump}])` → `RunResult{t, s, v, a, arrivals, departures, energy_traction_kwh, energy_regen_kwh, front_limit}`, plus `leg_times()`. It is a pure-Python time-step loop (dt 0.2 s).
- **Driver:** `Driver` (`dynamics.py:71-106`), with signalling presets in `operations.SIGNALLING` (none, lineside, cab, sacem, cbtc, etcs2, lzb).
- **Extras:** padding by `apply_padding`; eco allowance by `operations.eco_driver` (a bisection of up to 15 re-runs); `monte_carlo` (N re-runs, p50/p85/p95).
- **Dwell:** `MetroDwell` (door cycle, classes minor, standard, busy, transfer, terminal) and `CommuterDwell` (platform type, crew/button/auto doors, classes minor, standard, busy, hub, terminal), in `dwell.py`.
- **Outputs** (Line 6, measured):
  - `summary.json` (28 KB): `{program, line, train, warnings, directions[{direction, distance_km, stops, running_time_min, dwell_total_min, trip_time_min, scheduled_time_min, average_speed_kmh, max_speed_kmh, energy_*, stations[{name, km, arrive_s, depart_s, dwell_s, scheduled_arrive_s}]}], curves[{radius_m, spiral_m, start_km, end_km, segment, sketch, lat, lon}] (47), speed_zones[{from_km, to_km, mph, kmh}] (40)}`;
  - `stations_<dir>.csv`;
  - `trace_<dir>.csv` (`t_s, s_m, v_kmh, a_ms2`, about 300 KB each, capped at 20k rows);
  - `alignment.kmz` (75 KB), and PNG plots unless `--no-plots`.
- **Per segment:** run time per segment is only `arrive[k] - depart[k-1]`. There is no breakdown into accelerating, cruising and braking.
- **Not found in railsim:** a "where the time goes" breakdown, blocking time, minimum headway. `--headway` only prints the fleet needed: `ceil(cycle / headway)`.

## 7. Draft route-builder contract (railsim side)

What `Simulator` consumes is exactly `TrackData` plus stops. A route builder from solved sections can skip steps 1-8 of section 3.

**RouteInput** (`service`, `direction`, `year`, `scenario`):
- `sections[]`: `{section_id, version, dir: +1|-1, offset0, offset1}` in travel order. Chainage s is continuous across sections; reversals are marked with a jump.
- **Grid arrays** (2 m, per checklist 2.1.13): `s`, `x`/`y` (one region CRS; the plan measures in EPSG:26916), `rail_z`, `ground_z`, `grade`, `curvature` (signed 1/m, spirals included), `cant`.
- `curves[]`: `{s_ts, s_sc, s_cs, s_st, radius, spiral, cant, deflection, direction, rule_ids}`. `speed.curve_speed` needs R, cant, spiral and deflection.
- `structures[]`: `{s0, s1, kind ∈ {viaduct, at-grade, cut, fill, cut-and-cover, shield, deep-tube, sunk-box, tunnel}, method, form, rule_ids}`. The kind gives the tunnel flag.
- `stops[]`: `{station_id, legacy_name, s_centre, platform_length_m, platform: high|low, kind: island|side, psd, class, reverse_stub, can_stop}`.
- `fixed_restrictions[]`: `{s0, s1, v_ms, reason, rule_id}`; `turnouts[]` `{s, spec, diverging}`; `neutral_sections[]`; `supply_zones[]`; `operating_zones[]` `{s0, s1, zone_id}` (this replaces regex on KMZ segment names).
- `issues[]`.

**ServiceOverlay** (from the service and era records):
- stop pattern, `dwell` class or override per stop, `train_key`, load factor;
- era overlay (signalling, driver, dwell, `code_steps`);
- padding, turnback, headway by period.

**Output per direction:**
- `legs[]`: `{from, to, run_s, dwell_s, arrive_s, depart_s, scheduled_s, vmax}`;
- speed curve `(t, s, v, a, limit)`;
- energy;
- restriction list with reasons (the attribution keys).

The legacy `build_route` stays only behind the KMZ import (`kml.py`, `LocalProjection`).

## 11. Analysis engine host (from the measurements above)

- **In-process Python.** One job is (service, direction, year, scenario, engine version), keyed by a content hash of the sections, service, era, rolling stock and code.
- **Inputs from the model only:** no KMZ, no network, no ground fetch. The solved section replaces `build_route`, the 1.4-21 s per run measured above.
- **Per-job cost:** about 0.15 s per direction for the simulation. Cost multiplies with:
  - eco allowance: up to 15 runs;
  - Monte Carlo: N runs;
  - marginal "where the time goes": one re-run per restriction, about 40 zones on Line 6. At 0.14 s that is roughly 6 s per direction; this is an estimate, not measured.
- **Process pool:** the loop is pure Python and bound by the interpreter lock (GIL), so parallel work across services needs processes, not threads.
- **Side effects to strip:**
  - argparse and printing in `app.main`;
  - writes to `output/<stem>`;
  - cache writes in `data/elevation_cache`;
  - module-level seed/repo path picks in `metro_profile.py:117-126`;
  - `_repo_root` resolution (`app.py:450`).

## What the new hub must support from this part

- **Solved section → `TrackData`:** a route builder over the 2 m arrays (rail z, grade, signed curvature, cant, structure kind → tunnel flag, limit) plus a curve list (R, spiral, cant, deflection) and a stop list with chainage, platform length, height, PSD and class.
- **Restrictions as records with rule ids and reasons:** turnouts, throats, terminal approaches, posted limits, neutral sections, supply zones, and operating/signalling zones by chainage range instead of KMZ name regex.
- **Era and fleet as records:** the 5 Metro and 5 RER eras with their overlays (signalling, driver, dwell, code steps, PSD), the fleet by year, the RER `through_from`, the 24 pre-through `history.services`, covered ways, and per-line PSD.
- **Services as records:** a route through sections, a stopping pattern (class-based patterns as APEx uses), years valid, a reference year, train, load, padding, turnback per terminal, headway by period, and a mission code field (no data exists yet; ruling needed). Configs must be generated, not hand-kept.
- **Rolling stock as records:** 28 trains with every field in the `rollingstock.py` docstring, including `te_curve`, `brake_curve`, tilt and `min_curve_radius`.
- **Outputs stored as results:** per-leg times, the speed/limit/acceleration trace, energy, warnings, the restriction attribution, the accelerating/cruising/braking split, and Monte Carlo and eco variants.
- **Legacy keys kept:** config stems (73 baseline run times), Profiles route ids (Line_*, RER_*, RER_[A-E]_*), chronology segment keys, Deck station keys, complex ids.
- **The RER-n ↔ TRI letter mapping:** numbered services over lettered routes.
- **Calibration gates:** MBTA Orange and Needham must stay within ±1 %.
- **Chronology as records:** per-segment (station-pair) and per-station opening year and month, phase override, per-service year overrides, `phase_years` bands, and the 41 routes with parent branches. The hub must filter sections and services by year from this.
- **Placeholders flagged:** the 54 TRI segments with year 1900 and the self-pair keys.
- **Station elements as typed records:** level (depth), part (kind, era, op, polygon), flight (kind, top, bottom, start, end, width, units), lift (levels), fare line, entrance (kind, level), track, cavern. Each has an era tag (including the undated 'B').
- **Station frame transforms:** the 11°-turned station frame and the north-up region frame.
- **A weighted walking graph** built from those records, with lengths, widths, fare lines and step-free flags; today's graph is topology only.
- **Station access, platform type, station type and complex records** as they stand in `hub/seed/params`.
- **3D fed from model records,** not from headless pages: line samples (rail and type every 5 m), tracks at structure spacing, platforms at rail + 1.0 m, context structures, ground tiles. The issue list and the snapshot's validated schema should become typed records.
- **A runner with no side effects:** no internet, no writes into the repo, results in the DB/cache. Inputs must come from the hub's records (Ben's `hub/seed` alignments), not the stale `data/profile` copies.
