# Cincinnati Rail: opening timeline

From the Chronology record (hub `chronology/segments`). Dates are opening dates; the month where it is set. A run is a line's segments that opened on the same date one after another; stations are listed with the run that reached them, later stations as infill. Shared track is listed once, under the first line that has it.

214 of 556 segments have a date; 326 are not set.

## 1930s

### 1934

- **Line 8** (Phase 2): Northside to Union Terminal (15 segments). Stations: Northside, Bruckman, Cincinnati State, Morrison, Burnet North, Burnet East, Daniels, Jefferson, Mt Auburn, Melindy, Ziegler, 13th, Music Hall, Laurel Park, Union Terminal. _Line 8 opening segment, Northside to Union Terminal (tracker 2B)_

### 1936

- **Line 9** (Phase 2): Camp Washington to Oakley (16 segments). Stations: Camp Washington, Hopple, Dixmyth, VA Medical Center, UC Medical/Corryville, Bathgate, Beecher, Chapel, Woodburn, Scarborough, Grandin, Withrow, Wasson, Oakley Square, Appleton, Oakley. _Line 9 opening segment, Camp Washington to Oakley (tracker 2B)_

### 1937

- **Line 10** (Phase 2): Mill Creek to Paradrome (11 segments). Stations: Mill Creek, Garrard, Wilstach, Liberty, Warner, Republic, Cumber, Elsinore, Cincinnati Art Museum, Paradrome. _Line 10 opening segment, Mill Creek to Paradrome (tracker 2B)_

### 1939

- **Line 11** (Phase 2): Manhattan to Bullock (14 segments). Stations: Manhattan, Clay, Dayton, Van Voast, Prospect, Patchen, Mansion Hill, Levee, Taylor, Arsenal, Park Place, Mutter Gottes, Bakewell, Scholl, Bullock. _Line 11 opening segment, Manhattan (Bellevue) to Bullock (tracker 2B; Ben chose Bullock for the tracker's "Ludlow", which is on Lines 8 and 2b)_

## 1940s

Stations: Marshall, Stratford/Hughes, Burnet, Walnut Hills, McMillan, Taft, Hoffman, Hewitt, Alliance Academy, Burwood, Edmonson, Robertson, Maverick, Ridge, Pleasant Ridge. _Line 12 opening segment, Hopple to Pleasant Ridge (tracker 2B)_

## 1960s

### 1969

- **CVG Express** (Later): Union Terminal to Terminal 1A (5 segments). Stations: Union Terminal, Terminal 3, Terminal 2, Terminal 4, Terminal 1B, Terminal 1A. _CVG Express opened 1969 (Ben); built as a later extension_
