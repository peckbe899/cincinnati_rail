CUT approaches

Arch viaduct (Ben): the six APEx tracks run a set height (6.5 m) above the deck cross streets from Marshall to W 6th, so every street passes under. CUT's upper platforms rise to about 160.3 m. The four lower-level tracks ride the viaduct from Ludlow to Bank, then dive between Bank and Findlay and pass under Findlay into the deck, about 550 m from the lower platforms. Bank is lowered and Findlay raised 2 m to keep both open; Dayton, Exeter and York end at the dive.

North trunk (14 October, Ben: every path on one viaduct, Ludlow to CUT): west to east, Transvalley/R5, APEx Dayton/Norwood (4), APEx Indianapolis (2), TRI B/D and ORR/intercity E-H. Rails reach the ground at Ludlow (155.5 m), whose 1922 viaduct goes over them, and climb 0.79% to 166.6 m at the deck tip, then hold 6.5 m over the lobe streets. Hopple comes down to deck level and passes under. TRI B/D ride the viaduct from Ludlow: Arlington (B) on the east side at 164.6 m, centred on the KMZ point; D leaves B north of Camp Washington by a flyover (southbound D on the west of B, northbound D on the east, crossing over B as it climbs); Camp Washington (B, D) on the viaduct at about 165.2 m; then 3.3% down south of it into a box under the deck to Straight (151.6 m), bored from Straight. Peak service on the shared pair: B every 4 min, D every 8 min (22.5 trains an hour each way). The Indianapolis pair and D climb 2.5% on one deck from s 3,644 to 174.3 m and turn west on the KMZ curve over the Dayton/Norwood and Transvalley/R5 tracks, then over RER A (167.0 m) as ruled. R5 leaves the west pair for the Cumminsville Spur (its levels west of the trunk are not set). ORR E moves 22 m east from Camp Washington to Arlington to make room for B/D. At the dive Garrard line is raised 1.8 m onto the box roof, Rawson lowered 1.2 m and Township 2.8 m to pass under the ramp; Meeker and New street N end at it. North of Ludlow the at-grade layout is not set and B is drawn on its KMZ line.

All eight lower platforms are 305 m, centred under the APEx field. The APEx crosses the Ohio on its own bridge just east of the CS bridge, where the APEx Routes KMZ puts it. A-D feed the CS pair, which merges with the CVG Express pair at the CS bridge; E-H feed the C&O pair, which stays at -2 under W 6th and then climbs onto the TRIMET C&O viaduct. A freight passing track bypasses the platforms on the west side, as in 1955.

TRI A passes under the lower level at or below 135.9 m (Ben: accepted); TRI B/D can share its level at the Union Terminal TRI box, or sit above it. Open: Lines 7, 8 and 10 not profiled; Line 4 between Garrard and Chinatown against the CS pair.

Sources

Deck base: Sanborn 1904 Cincinnati, vol. 1 sheets 33-35, 47, 57-58, 67-69, 77; vol. 3 sheets 201-202, 211-212, 221-222, 233-234, 245-246, 253-254; vol. 5 sheets 460 (upper panel), 461-464, 466-468, 491-496, 504-505, 511-514, 519-520, 522. Since 13 October the deck sheets are fitted by the basin method: the street crossings on every sheet, tied to the neighbouring sheets, to surviving OSM corners and to the 1912 city survey, in one adjustment with the north sheets (ties median 0.8 m, OSM 1.7 m, 1912 survey 2.1 m). The first deck fit had hung sheets 33-35 and 47 on very few points and placed them 13-20 m too far west (57 by 8-10 m); the 1922 atlas, the 1912 survey and the surviving piece of Horne Street agree on the new positions, and Garrard and Horne moved east with them. Lower Price Hill block (State Ave to Western Ave, Gest St to the river): the 1904 sheets are bent onto the 1922 atlas, vol. 1 plates 3, 6, 26 and 29, with a thin-plate spline through the crossings both atlases print (held within 0.3 m), easing back to the plain fit over the last 80 m before Gest and Western Ave; the creek seam between the vol. 5 and vol. 1 sheets is gone. One corner is left as fitted: at the creek mouth by W 6th, sheet 34 and 1922 plate 3 draw the creek bend about 35 m east of where the 1912 survey has it, with no crossing within 150 m to settle it. Deck streets that carry a West End street across the yards start where that street's basin centreline meets the deck edge and keep its line as far as the Garrard line. The Garrard line, fitted to the 1904 sheets, is moved 9 m west as a whole (13 October), as Garrard was never a set street across the yards: it now runs into Camp Washington's Garrard Avenue at Township (0.9 m off its line; the 1904 sheets put the avenue 8.7 to 9.4 m west of the old fit). There each bends, by up to about 1.2 degrees, onto the boulevard's square (93.64 degrees), so every street west of Garrard meets the boulevard at a right angle; the two grids meet along Garrard, as the downtown grids meet along Market Street in San Francisco. Gest bends onto the grid too and meets State Avenue 11 m north of its 1904 corner, since the upper part of Lower Price Hill is cleared for the Exposition. W 8th (the viaduct line) fronts surviving 1904 blocks, so it is straightened only part way, as a long-lived street would be: one straight run from Garrard to State on a bearing halfway between its old line and the grid (93.94 degrees), moving its south side 1.5 to 2.5 m and its corner at State 4.8 m south. Its crossings west of Garrard are 0.3 degrees off square, and Line 4 stays under it (at most 5.7 m off the centreline; State station 14.3 m, inside the 33.5 m street). North of Bank the deck streets continue Dolph, McRoberts and Western Avenue, and Harrison's re-aligned avenue meets Harrison at the deck edge. Dayton, Exeter and York stop at the outer wall on both sides of the lower pairs' open cut.

North (13 October): Camp Washington, Northside and South Cumminsville, vol. 3 sheets 235-236, 247, 255, 272-275, 279-282, 287-289, 293-295, 301-305 and vol. 6 sheets 601-635 (605, with no crossings, is not placed), fitted in the same adjustment. North of OSM's extract the control is 420 street corners read off the 1912 survey; 19 streets the 1904 sheets name differently from the 1912 survey (Zwick, which the survey prints Zinck; Dorman, now Powers) are matched by hand. Streets and blocks are built as in the basin. Basin sheets 223 and 224 (Brighton) were refitted with them: they had sat 5-17 m south at Harrison Avenue.

1922 additions (13 October): where no 1904 sheet reaches, the 1922 atlas (vol. 1, plate halves 16R, 34L, 34R, 37L and 37R) is laid under the 1904 sheets: the west bank between sheets 522 and 607-608, the creek and railroads north of Hopple, and Camp Washington's strip east of the old canal bed. The plates are fitted on crossings shared with the 1904 sheets, OSM corners and the 1912 survey (medians 2.8-3.2 m at the controls). Streets drawn from them: the strip between the canal bed and West McMicken south of Marshall (Halstead, both pieces of Klottman, West McMillan, Hukill, Eliza, Rush, McMicken itself, and Straight and Marshall carried on to McMicken), Gene Alley, Meeker, Burlington and Ledlie; and two crossings to the west side: Hopple Street, a viaduct over the deck's north lobe and Mill Creek to Beekman on the line of the 1904 "Hopple (Bridge Av.)", and Ludlow Avenue from Knowlton's Corner (Dodsworth Av on the 1904 sheet) across Mill Creek, the B&O and the canal bed to Lafayette Avenue. Central Parkway now follows OSM north through Camp Washington to Ludlow Avenue, 90 ft wide on the old canal bed (150 ft in the basin).

Lower Price Hill (13 October, option C as Ben ruled): north of W 8th the blocks are cleared for the deck and the Exposition, so the basin's W 9th, Richmond, Court, Oehler, Clark and Hopkins now cross the boulevard and run west to Depot (Hopkins to Summer), against Lower Price Hill's north-south streets Depot, Summer, Berlin and Evans. Those four are drawn straight and parallel to the boulevard (best fit through the 1904 centres, moved 1.0 to 3.4 m), so the crossings north of W 8th are right angles. W 9th and Richmond meet Depot 8.4 m and 6.2 m south of Dutton and South, which go on to State: the strip between State and Depot keeps its 1904 streets, and the new grid meets it with a jog. Gest runs straight on the grid from State Avenue to Garrard, then on the West End's Gest. South of W 8th, Lower Price Hill's own 1904 grid (State, Depot, Burns, Neave, Hatmaker, Staebler, Storrs, St. Michael, German, Price, Evans and W 6th to Burns) is drawn from the vol. 5 sheets like the basin, with Hatmaker, Staebler, Storrs, St. Michael, Price, Dutton and South set straight on the boulevard's square and Burns (W 6th to Hatmaker) and Depot parallel to the boulevard; no crossing moves more than 2.5 m, inside the sheets' fitting error (Evans' corner at W 8th moves 3.7 m with the straight Evans north of it), and State, Neave, German, Evans and W 6th keep their surveyed bends; the covered C.H.&D. curve stays one long deck block, with Perin the only street across to the boulevard. Between Evans and the boulevard the new blocks run about 290 m, as the 1904 marsh had no street there. Crossing levels (LiDAR ground, the draft deck and APEx levels, TRIMET_Lines.kmz): Hopple has to stand at 165.3 m or more over the APEx ramp (158.5 m); it is drawn as a viaduct from Meeker over Spring Grove (4.4% down to the landing) and climbs 1.0% from Beekman. The TRIMET trunk (RER B and D) is at 151.3 m under the deck at Hopple, and RER D's viaduct, which falls about 1.8% from Westwood, reaches the trunk 250 m north of Hopple, so it sets neither crossing. Ludlow crosses the TRIMET north trunk at 155.5 m, at grade, so it rises about 3.5% from Knowlton's Corner to 162.3 m or more and is a viaduct for its first 400 m.

Exposition Boulevard (13 October): the boulevard on the culverted creek line is named for the 1913 Exposition, for which Mill Creek was decked over.

West side of the valley (13 October): the land between Exposition Boulevard and State Avenue and Beekman Street, from Hopkins to Ralston Avenue, is laid out in three parts. Exposition grounds, Hopkins to Harrison: the West End streets from Kenner to McRoberts cross the boulevard on its square and run to State Avenue or, north of Liberty, to Wallace Street, the 1904 Wallace carried north along the foot of the hill to State at Ernst; Hopkins runs on from Summer to State and W Liberty to State; Summer, Berlin and Evans are carried north to Wallace, and New street W runs midway between Evans and the boulevard. Harrison to Hopple: Queen City and Harrison's re-aligned avenue run on to their 1904 corners on Beekman, and Tremont, Waverly and Knox are carried east from Beekman to Johnson Street. North of the Palace grounds the streets are Camp Washington's own: Alfred, Straight and Marshall on their 1904 bearings from Garrard to Beekman (Alfred meets Beekman at Fairmount), Township and Willow Lane carried west from their 1922 ends (41 m apart, so the blocks between are one lot deep, as east of Garrard), one new street midway between Marshall and Township, and Johnson, Rawson and Meeker carried south on Garrard's bearing (Johnson from Harrison, passing under RER A's viaduct just south of Alfred), with New streets J1 to J3 parallel to Johnson every 100 m toward Beekman. Hopple to Ralston (Millvale bottoms): Camp Washington's streets from Stock to Whiteley are carried west on 93.6 degrees to Beekman, and Follett, Roll, Dawson and Borden south on their own bearings. State Avenue (Gest to Harrison), the 1904 Harrison (State to Beekman), Beekman (1904 line, OSM north of Hopple), Cummings Street and the Vinton and Denham stubs are drawn in the basin layer from the picked 1904 crossings. Rail lines cut streets where they cross at or near street level (5.8 m clear plus 1.0 m structure; 6.5 m under the APEx viaduct, as ruled for it; the portal rule over RER boxes): RER A's ramp cuts Dayton, Bank and Dolph west of the boulevard (Dayton and Bank keep stubs from Wallace; Dolph and the narrow strip north of it end at the boulevard), while Exeter rides the box roof (rail 5.2 m down). Since 14 October the north trunk is a viaduct from Ludlow to CUT; since 15 October it runs straight from Ethan to Brashears, centred between New streets J1 and J2 as extended on the page (2.3 m from each street to the structure edge, ORR E moving in only north of Brashears), with curves of about 435 m radius to Camp Washington's platforms and 740 m toward Ludlow. So the lobe streets (Township, Willow Lane, Johnson, Stock, Ethan, Bates, Alabama, Monmouth) and the Millvale streets north of the tip pass under it; only TRI B/D's dive between Camp Washington and Straight cuts streets: New street N and Meeker end at it, Garrard line is raised 1.8 m over the box, Rawson lowered 1.2 m and Township 2.8 m under the ramp. Off the deck, blocks are building blocks; the open rail corridors carry no blocks.

RER A (13 October, Ben: N2, 3.3%, Westwood at ground): 135.9 m under CUT's lower level, 147.5 m at Evans under W Liberty and 148.4 m where it leaves the deck north of York; boxed under Exeter, then a walled ramp at 3.3% beside the boulevard to the fork at 159.3 m. The Westwood branch turns 62.6 degrees on a 200 m curve, crosses the foot of State Avenue at 162.5 m (8.8 m clear) and climbs 3.26% to its platform at ground, 168.9 m. The Northside branch stays on a viaduct: Western Hills elevated, 165.2 m over Harrison's avenue, 168.2 m over the Hopple viaduct (6.8 m), easing to 167.0 m under RER D (174.3 m) and holding 167.0 m through South Cumminsville and Northside, both elevated; every street from Queen City to Mad Anthony passes under. From the Northside platform end it drops at 3.3%: Chambers, Lingo and Dane end at its walls, Linden crosses over the box (rail 144.2 m, 5.9 m down), and the line goes into the tunnel under Spring Grove Cemetery (not profiled beyond).

Basin: streets and blocks of the West End, Over-the-Rhine, the CBD and the riverfront, by the deck method. 154 Sanborn sheets (vol. 1 sheets 1-83, vol. 2 sheets 101-156, vol. 3 sheets 201-227) are fitted in one least-squares adjustment on 2,640 street intersections read off the sheets: ties between sheets (median 0.6 m), surviving OSM corners (median 1.0 m; streets widened since, such as Linn, Liberty and Eggleston, left out) and the 1912 survey for demolished corners at low weight, with the deck sheets held fixed. Each street is drawn as straight runs between its intersections at the width printed on the sheets (10 ft for unmarked alleys, 40 ft for the few unmarked streets), and the blocks are the spaces left between the streets. Neighbouring runs that agree within 3 m are drawn as one straight line, a street that jogs at a crossing steps inside that crossing, and a width printed for one side of a crossing changes there. Central Parkway, Eggleston Avenue and Reading Road follow today's OSM centrelines. Names and spellings are as printed. Checked against OpenStreetMap: on streets that survive unwidened, the centrelines sit a median 0.7 m from today's (85% within 2 m, 94% within 3 m). The check re-registered sheet 18, fixed 11 misplaced streets, removed 9 links that ran through 1904 buildings, and added 35 streets and alleys that the sheets show and OSM still has, drawn on the OSM line. The strip east of CUT between Kenner and Hopkins is left open for a ruling on the forecourt.

Metro and RER: Metro alignments and stations from the Metro line files (Ben's KMZ), RER from Cincinnati Rail.kmz (RER A's Westwood branch as designed on 13 October), Fountain Square RER platforms from Ben's platform KMZs. Zoomed in, each line shows its tracks and platforms: platforms 500 ft for the Metro and 670 ft for the RER; Lines 1-7 as twin tubes about 8 m apart with island platforms, Lines 8-14 as one double-track bore with side platforms; RER with island platforms. Track spacing and platform widths are drawing values. Union Terminal (TRI A, B and D) is set by direction as at Châtelet-Les Halles, with two tracks per island: westbound on the north side (A and B on one island, D westbound on the centre island), eastbound on the south (D eastbound, then B and A on one island), so A and B change cross-platform in the same direction. B and D arrive together from Western and split in the station; east of it D's tracks join A's to City Hall and B leaves on its own pair. B/D run at grade under the deck to Camp Washington, in a dug tunnel on the KMZ line through Straight, and bored from there: one curve into Straight (450 m radius, as the KMZ), then a 2.0 km straight to a 250 m radius bend onto the station axis 330 m west of the station centre, so A westbound crosses them about 280 m beyond the platforms. The bore drops the old freight-yard trace past Western and its S-bend; Western now sits on the straight between Dolph and Western. The layout needs crossings at separate levels west of the station (A westbound across B/D) and east of it (three, all B against A/D); they are marked, and direction labels show when zoomed in. Editing (14 October): in Edit mode the Metro and RER centre lines move and bend like streets; a line edited, or with a station changed, has its platforms drawn along it (canon length, island or side, slid along the line but always reaching the station point) and its tracks redrawn as a pair. Stations can be deleted. Edits are saved with the layout and applied when Claude regenerates the layers.

CUT tracks: APEx lines from the APEx Routes KMZ; CS bridge clearance 78.9 ft (Waterway Guide), rail taken as about 164-166 m.
