Plate {N}: crossings

Pick a crossing from the list or the small map. The big view is the plate at full size: drag to move, click to put your own point. Red square = reader A, green circle = reader B, blue + = today's crossing through the current fit, purple × = your point. Two questions per crossing: is it drawn on the plate, and if so where. Where the same two streets meet at several points (a split intersection), it is one crossing: all of today's points and all the readers' points are shown, and Both keeps A's and B's points as two real points. A crossing the list does not have: Add a crossing.
