Controls

Wheelzoom   drag empty ground to move
Clickselect a line (Shift+click adds)
Double-clickselect the whole street the line belongs to
Shift+dragselect every line in a box
1 3 0correct / street side with no printed line / put back as it was
Drag an endmove that end of the selected line
Drag the linemove the whole selected line
Arrowsnudge selected 1 px (Shift: 5)
Cclose corners. Two lines selected: both are stretched or trimmed to the point where they cross. One line, or a box of lines: every end is taken to the nearest line it almost meets (within 60 px)
Jjoin the selected pieces of one straight side into a single line
Snappingan end you drag or draw jumps to a nearby end or line (yellow ring). Hold Alt to place it freely
Bink bins: colour every black mark by what the rules guess it is. Tick bins on and off in the box; nothing in that layer can be edited yet
Ndraw: click two points; Esc to stop
Deldelete the selected lines: any line, not only yours
Ctrl+Zundo
Every change is saved at once. A line you leave alone stays as it is.
