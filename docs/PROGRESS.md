# Progress tracker

Living list of what is done and what is left. Newest changes at the top of each section.

## Done

- **Repository layout**: `railsim/` package with two programs sharing one core:
  - `python -m railsim.metro <config>` for metro lines, with a door-cycle dwell model and ATO-style driving.
  - `python -m railsim.commuter <config>` for commuter and RER lines, with platform-type dwell and engineer-style driving.
  - The legacy notebooks are kept in `legacy/` (outputs stripped).
- **KMZ reading** (`kml.py`):
  - Keeps the folder hierarchy.
  - Expands MultiGeometry.
  - Reads altitudes.
  - Needs no simplekml.
- **Track network and routing** (`network.py`), which replaces the pairwise stitcher:
  - Snaps segment endpoints together.
  - Detects T-junctions.
  - Routes through stations with turn constraints, so a route never reverses through a junction.
  - Handles branched RER folders. Tested on all Cincinnati RER folders, the Orange Line and Needham.
- **Horizontal alignment reconstruction** (`horizontal.py`): turns the hand-drawn polyline into real geometry (tangents, spirals, arcs).
  - Spiral lengths follow the AREMA rules L = 1.63·Eu·V and L = 62·Ea.
  - Reverse curves get a tangent of at least 2 s of running.
  - Curves drawn with many vertices are merged and fitted at their best-fit radius. Single corners use the largest radius within an offset tolerance.
  - Removes drawing jitter (Douglas-Peucker).
  - Track length is the true curve length, not the polygon length.
- **Vertical profile design** (`profile.py`): fits a designed track profile to the ground profile.
  - Grades are bounded and platforms are level within 0.5%.
  - Vertical curves are smoothed.
  - Going below ground costs more than going above it.
  - Each point is classified as at-grade, fill, viaduct, cut or tunnel.
- **Elevation sources** (`elevation.py`):
  - The legacy CSV.
  - Altitudes stored in the KMZ.
  - USGS 3DEP bare-earth data via EPQS, with a disk cache. This needs internet; it is blocked in the build sandbox but works on a normal machine.
- **Posted speed limits** (`speed.py`):
  - Curve speeds use cant plus cant deficiency, with G = 1500 mm.
  - Short spirals are limited by the rate of change of cant deficiency.
  - A curve shorter than a car is treated as averaged over the car's length.
  - Limits are posted in 5 mph steps.
  - Short zones are consolidated into their neighbours.
  - User restrictions and terminal approaches can be added.
- **Physics engine** (`dynamics.py`): force-based and time-stepped.
  - Tractive effort is limited by adhesion, maximum effort and power.
  - Resistance uses the Davis equation, with a tunnel factor.
  - Grade and curve resistance (Röckl) are averaged over the train's length.
  - Rotating mass is included.
  - Acceleration and braking changes are jerk-limited.
  - Braking follows a braking curve that allows for brake build-up delay.
  - Restrictions hold until the rear of the train clears them.
  - Traction energy and regenerated energy are tracked.
- **Dwell models** (`dwell.py`):
  - Metro: door cycle per TCQSM.
  - Commuter: level vs low-platform boarding, crew-operated doors, conductor and brake release.
- **Timetable layer**:
  - Padding as a percentage plus a final-leg recovery time.
  - Compares against reference schedules.
  - Writes CSV, JSON, KMZ (alignment coloured by speed limit, plus curves, structures and stations) and plots.
- **Unit tests** (`tests/`): analytic run times, restrictions held over the train length, power-limited and uphill runs, jerk limit, curve recovery, routing, profile limits.
- **Research notes**: track geometry and commuter rolling stock.
- **Calibration data**: MBTA GTFS (Fall 2025) gives station coordinates and schedules for the Orange Line and Needham.

- **Calibration** (see `docs/CALIBRATION.md`):
  - Orange Line: model 35.1 min vs GTFS 35 min, with no tuning.
  - Needham: 33.5 min technical / 38.0 min padded vs 40 min published. The remaining gap is explained by unpublished branch speed limits.
- **Metro library**:
  - MBTA Orange Line CRRC (VE-10-036 spec) and Blue Line No. 5.
  - CMCC Types 1903, 1925, 1948, 1985 and 2005 (48 ft x 10 cars, hill-geared for 5%).
  - Long-car Types 1985L and 2005L (60 ft x 8 cars), barred from curves under 65 m (route-availability warning).
- **RER library**:
  - Brightliner, Silverliner, Swiftliner, and the A to F sets from their real inspirations.
  - Reference consists: MBTA HSP46 + 6 bilevels, SC-44 + 6 bilevels, Caltrain KISS.
- **Cincinnati configs**:
  - 15 metro lines and 31 RER through-routes, generated by `tools/make_cincinnati_configs.py`.
  - The generator drops branch pairs that would need a reversal, and disambiguates same-named stations (two "Dover"s).
  - All 46 run.
- **Cab-signal code speeds**: limits round down to the code steps.
- **Manual-driving creep**: a slow final approach into platforms.
- **Padding**:
  - UIC-style minutes per 100 km.
  - Pathing time at single-track meets.
- **Tools**:
  - `railsim.batch` runs whole folders.
  - `tools/sensitivity.py` runs what-if variants.
- **Curve-fitting fixes found during calibration**:
  - Drawn chord chains are merged whenever one circle fits them (fixed the Back Bay curve).
  - A 3 m jitter tolerance removes wiggles a few metres in size.
  - Junction turns are allowed up to 90°.
  - Off-track terminals are snapped to the track with a warning.

- **Simulator survey** (`docs/research/simulators.md`), now implemented:
  - Signalling presets: lineside, cab, CBTC, ETCS L2 and LZB.
  - Driver profiles with OSRD-style anticipation/postponement offsets, plus brake and power fractions.
  - Curtius-Kniffler adhesion with wet and leaf states, affecting both traction and braking.
  - Tabulated tractive-effort curves (the SUMO ICE 3 table for the Velaro D).
  - Speed-dependent HSR braking.
  - Diesel power ramp and auxiliary load.
  - Supply-voltage power factor and neutral sections.
  - Turnout-number speeds.
  - Head stops at the far end of the platform.
  - MARECO-like eco allowance with energy savings.
  - Monte Carlo p50/p85/p95.
  - Turnback cycle time and fleet size.
- **Train program presets** (`preset: intercity|hsr`) and `python -m railsim.train`.
  - New HSR stock: Velaro D, ETR 1000, AGV, Avelia Liberty.
  - Demo: `configs/train/example_hsr_needham_alignment.yaml`.
- **Unit-aware config merging**: a `_kmh` value replaces a `_mph` default.
- **Calibration after these changes**: Orange Line +0.4%; Needham −5% padded. The cab preset follows OSRD and adds no offsets.

- **Operating zones** (`operating_zones` in a config): a section can use its own signalling and driver, matched by drawn segment name or by station range. Example: CBTC with ATO on an RER "Trunk".
- **Operating eras** (`data/operations/*.yaml`, `--year`). The year picks the fleet, signalling, GoA and dwell settings:
  - **Metro:**
    - 1905: block signals with gate cars.
    - 1916: pneumatic doors.
    - 1986: fixed-block cab ATC with ATO (GoA2).
    - 2006: CBTC (GoA2).
    - 2020 onward: CBTC GoA3 (DTO).
  - **RER:**
    - Lineside, then cab signals.
    - SACEM on the trunk from 1985.
    - CBTC + ATO on the trunk from 2006.
  - Values come from `docs/research/eras.md`, e.g. IRT 1904 express stops of about 30 s, WMATA auto-doors, DLR GoA3 door practice.
- **Fixed-block code quantization** (`driver.block_length_m`): a lower code is only received at block boundaries, and a higher one returns one block after the restriction is cleared.
  - Line 6: CBTC is 7% faster than fixed-block ATC, against 6% measured on the Jubilee line.
  - Orange Line: +2.3% with 300 m blocks.
- **ATO margins come from the signalling system** (CBTC 3 km/h, SACEM 2 km/h) unless `ato_margin_*` is set. Human drivers keep 1 mph (metro) or 2 mph (commuter).
- **Stub terminals and loops:**
  - A route entry `{name: X, reverse: true}` sends the train to the buffer stop, where the driver changes ends.
    - Minimum dwell is `stations.reversal_min_s`, default 240 s.
    - The front moves on by a train length when the train reverses.
    - The alignment is fitted in pieces, so the fold is not read as a hairpin curve.
  - Loop services start and finish at the same station.
  - Track drawn twice is merged.
- **APEx national HSR** (`tools/make_apex_configs.py`):
  - All 22 routes generate.
  - Stations get Limited/Express/Local classes from their folders; select a pattern with `--stops limited|express|all`.
  - Reversals at St Louis (Transcontinental, City of New Orleans) and Chicago (Pioneer Ambassador).
  - Lone Star runs as a loop.
- **APEx Gen 6 train**: physics updated from CR450, Frecciarossa 1000 and E5 research. Resistance is about 82 kN at 400 km/h, braking 0.8 m/s² below 200 km/h and 0.55 m/s² at 400 km/h. Canon tilt is kept.
- **Tests**: 25, now covering zones, eras, tilt, fixed blocks, reversals and duplicate track.

- **RER is driver-only in every era** (Paris RER practice): the driver releases the doors, passengers open them with a button, and the driver closes them and departs after a mirror or CCTV check. Set with `dwell.door_control: crew|button|auto`. US commuter crews (Needham) keep the conductor model.
- **Metro platform screen doors by line.** Every line is GoA3 from 2020. Lines 1, 2, 2b, 3, 5, 7, 8 and 10 have PSDs at every station, adding 3 s per stop (`psd_lines` in the metro era file). Per-station overrides: `stations.info.<name>.psd`.
- **Services before through-running** (`history` and `through_from` in `data/operations/cincinnati_rer.yaml`). Every line ran into Union Terminal and terminated there; trains could continue as a new service after stopping, but there were no through lines. Through-running dates: RER-1 1985 (crosstown tunnel), RER-2 and RER-3 1988 (RER-3 upgraded for SACEM), RER-4 1990, RER-5 1996 (new deep-bore tunnel). Older-era services run without the trunk signalling zone.
  - 20 generated `cin_rer_*_pre_rer_*` configs, each with `years:` and a default `year:`.
    - RER-1 west into Union Terminal, on the yard approaches digitized from your screenshot (`tools/digitize_historic.py` → `data/kmz/cincinnati_historic.kml`).
    - RER-1 east into Times.
    - RER-2, RER-4 and RER-5 into Union Terminal.
    - RER-5 from CVG over the Crescent Springs line from 1947.
  - Northgate and the RER-2/RER-3 CVG branches open with their line's through-running.
  - Configs refuse a `--year` outside their `years`.
- **Route tools**:
  - `track.extra` adds track from other folders or files.
  - `{via: [lat, lon]}` adds a non-stop routing waypoint.
  - `profile.structures` can match drawn segment names, e.g. tunnels.
- **RER-3 covered way** from Sedamsville to Riverbend/Belterra, modelled as tunnel in every RER-3 config (`covered_ways` in the RER era file).

- **APEx curve model corrected to canon** (`docs/research/geometry.md`):
  - Cant deficiency is banded: 130 mm up to 186 mph, 100 mm above.
  - Gen 6 no longer tilts. Only Gen 6T (8° tilt, 270 mm total, locked above 186 mph) tilts, on the canon tilt routes: Cardinal, Broadway Limited, Floridian, Transcontinental.
  - Tests reproduce the canon radius/speed table.

- **APEx finished vs sketched alignments** (`data/operations/apex.yaml`):
  - 133 of 305 drawn segments are marked finished, from your list of 19 corridors; they follow the drawing.
  - Every other segment is a sketch: each curve gets the largest radius within 250 m of the drawing, as an engineer smoothing it would (`track.finished`, `geometry.sketch_tolerance_m`).
  - Curves in `summary.json` now record their drawn segment, sketch flag and location.
