# North Viaduct Plan (proposal, 25 October 2026)

Status: agreed layout, not yet installed in the Mill Creek Deck page (page still at v126, DB layout version 2302). Geometry: `trunk2/geo4.py`, trunk north of Arlington `trunk2/lud19.npy`, drawings `trunk2/draw4.py`.

Offsets are + east of the trunk, s is KMZ chainage from CUT. 4.5 m between neighbouring tracks; RER islands 14.4 m between track centres; deck edge 4.2 m outside the outer track.

## Order and levels

- CUT to the junction, west to east: ORR/TV west pair | RER B/D | APEx Indy | APEx Dayton | APEx Norwood | ORR/TV east pair.

- North (CUT approach) viaduct: one level, no interlining. West viaduct: upper level APEx Indy + RER D (D on the south side), R5 (ORR/TV) on the lower level under it.

## RER B/D tunnel entry

- Twin bores carry on Ben's Western straight from the Western north platform end, one curve each (R about 4,550 m, s 1,750 to 2,150), then parallel to the trunk at -30.15 / -15.75.

- Straight station is in the box under the west side (about 25 m west of its earlier position). Box ends s 2,556; open ramp at 3.3% to Camp Washington (s 2,964).

## ORR/TV west pair

- Tight against Indy (-20.25 / -15.75) from CUT to s 2,235, directly over the bores and the box.

- Out on one constant-radius curve (620 m, 615.5 m inner track) to -39.15 / -34.65 by s 2,550 (B still 12.9 m below in the box).

## D junction and west viaduct

- D leaves B just north of Camp Washington (s 3,200 to 3,420): D SB off B SB and D NB off B NB, both inward between the B tracks (no crossing), to -25.65 / -21.15; D climbs 2.5% from s 3,250.

- Indy climbs 2.5% from s 3,644; one 780 m deck curve from s 3,904 onto the Indianapolis KMZ straight (joined at 1,050 m along the KMZ line on its tangent). D joins the Indy curve on one 700 m curve (no reverse curve).

- R5 leaves the west pair at s 3,991 on a 600 m curve and runs on the lower level under the deck. R5 NB crosses the west pair's SB track on the level (Ben, 25 October).

- Arlington stays on its present position (s 4,044 to 4,248, B at -30.15 / -15.75); the deck passes over its south 60 m (Ben, 25 October).

## North of Arlington (Ben: straights and single curves, no S where avoidable)

- Trunk: Arlington straight to s 4,260; curve 1 right, R 1,310 m (s 4,260 to 4,928); straight through Ludlow (s 4,928 to 5,354; OH-75 east and the culvert west leave no room); curve 2 right, R 800 m (s 5,354 to 5,950); 43 m straight; curve 3 left, R 1,250 m (s 5,993 to 6,248), forced by the open creek bend and the bridge line; today's line from s 6,248 over today's Mill Creek bridge.

- West pair and B move 9 m east on one concentric curve each (1,200 to 1,223 m, s 4,273 to 4,880), the widest that keeps B NB 4.5 m from Dayton. Then constant width to the viaduct end: W -30.15 / -25.65, B SB -21.15, B NB -6.75 (island spacing held between Arlington and Northside), deck 58.8 m.

- Viaduct ends at the Northside / Spring Grove platform end (s 5,570); fill north. West pair and B SB move back in on one curve each (about 730 m) onto the 43 m straight.

- Mitchell step-outs unchanged (Ben, 25 October).

## Mill Creek

- Mill Creek is decked and underground south-west (downstream) of Albone Street (where Albone meets the creek, about s 5,830); open upstream. The trunk keeps at least 3 m plus 1.5 m clear of the culvert line, the open creek and OH-75.
