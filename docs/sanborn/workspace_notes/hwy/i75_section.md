## Mill Creek Deck page implementation (Ben, 22 October)

These are the decisions from the 22 October discussion, now on the deck page (version 100). The page has the detail.

- Millvale Boulevard is drawn on the page's own street lines (Ben's KMLs), not on this map's lanes.

- Central Parkway meets OH-75 at a continuous T on Albone Street's axis (OH-75 km 18.96, 438 m north-east of the Ludlow signal).
  - Central Parkway runs straight from Ludlow, 505 m, square with Albone at the T and at about 65 degrees to OH-75. The hill between Millvale, Ludlow and Central Parkway is regraded by up to about 27 m for it.
  - Southbound OH-75 never stops. Two protected left lanes run in a median widened to 7.5 m, and left-turners cross the southbound bus lane before the junction.
  - Central Parkway turns right from both lanes. The outer lane becomes a third northbound lane, which drops north-east of the T at a point not yet set.
  - Banned: Central Parkway to Millvale Boulevard south-west, the northbound right into Central Parkway (both would be turns of about 114 degrees), and left turns from Central Parkway onto Ludlow. Those trips use Ludlow, Colerain or Spring Grove.
  - The Albone tail is dropped.

- Ludlow Avenue stays at ground on its 1914 line, and the rail trunk rises over it.
  - The trunk is at 162.5 m over Ludlow and holds that level through the RER B Northside / Spring Grove platforms on fill.
  - It returns to ground about 900 m north of Ludlow.

- OH-74 ends at a signal on Colerain at Kirby.
  - Through traffic takes path A: Colerain to Millvale Boulevard. Colerain is 26.2 m from Blue Rock to Millvale, and 33.2 m from OH-74 to Blue Rock, where it also carries the BRT.
  - The BRT runs Colerain, Blue Rock, then Spring Grove to the Colerain stop on Millvale Boulevard. Blue Rock and Spring Grove are 21.6 m, with centre bus lanes and one lane each way.
  - RER-1b stays on viaduct over OH-74.

- OH-74 median from km 10.59 to 18.67 carries APEx in the centre with the RER D pair beside it, not TRI E.
  - The median is 29.7 m and the section 73.42 m.
  - On the deck page RER D was already 9.0 m from the APEx centre.
