Cincinnati Southern Bridge

The four-track replacement across the Ohio between Cincinnati and Ludlow, as opened about 1930: a two-hinged, spandrel-braced through arch on the Hell Gate pattern, 402.2 m between the towers and 100 m over the river at its crown, with masonry towers on solid ground at both banks and no pier in the river. Drawn on the canon ground.
