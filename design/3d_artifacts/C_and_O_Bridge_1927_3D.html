C&O Bridge, 1927

C&O Bridge, 1927

The C&O's four-track crossing between Cincinnati and Covington, as finished about 1927: two Ludendorff-type bridges side by side on shared river piers, each a two-hinged truss arch springing from the river piers between two through-truss side spans, with American trusses (Pratt webs, Parker side spans), three arch ribs, the middle one carrying both decks. Plain abutments, no towers. The approaches run from the tunnel portals under W 6th to Covington Union. Drawn on the canon ground.
